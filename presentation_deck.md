# 📊 Presentation Deck: MEA Global Indian Language Learning & Propagation Platform (VedSarthi)

> **Tagline**: Learn Indian Languages. Connect with India.  
> **Mandate**: Ministry of External Affairs (MEA), Government of India  

---

## 🖼️ Slide 1: Title Slide
- **Title**: VedSarthi — MEA Global Indian Language Learning & Propagation Platform
- **Subtitle**: A Sovereign, AI-Powered Platform for Learning, Practising, Assessing & Promoting Indian Languages Worldwide
- **Presented To**: Ministry of External Affairs (MEA), Government of India
- **Key Features**:
  - Sovereign Government Architecture
  - Phase 1 Hindi Focus + 21 Schedule VIII Language Packs
  - AI Placement Diagnostic & AI Voice Tutor
  - Global Mission & Embassy Outreach Governance

---

## 🖼️ Slide 2: Executive Mandate & Product Positioning
- **Primary Mission**:
  1. Propagate Indian languages to global scholars, diplomats, foreign envoys, and diaspora NRI youth.
  2. Connect international learners with India's rich linguistic and cultural heritage.
  3. Provide accredited CEFR (A1–C2) certification.
  4. Aggregate scattered national learning resources under a single sovereign portal.
- **Tagline**: *Learn Indian Languages. Connect with India.*
- **Core Pillars**: **Learn** (Adaptive AI) ➔ **Practice** (Voice Tutor) ➔ **Discover** (Resource Hub) ➔ **Connect** (Language Circles) ➔ **Govern** (Mission Outreach).

---

## 🖼️ Slide 3: Phased Language Strategy
- **Phase 1 — Hindi (Available Now)**:
  - Full CEFR A1-B2 pathways, Devanagari script training, formal diplomatic greetings, and interactive voice practice.
- **Modular Language Pack Architecture (Upcoming 21 Schedule VIII Languages)**:
  - *Assamese, Bengali, Bodo, Dogri, Gujarati, Kannada, Kashmiri, Konkani, Maithili, Malayalam, Marathi, Meitei, Nepali, Odia, Punjabi, Sanskrit, Santali, Sindhi, Tamil, Telugu, Urdu*.
- **Future Expansion (Track B)**:
  - Indian ➔ Foreign Language Learning (French, Spanish, German, Japanese, Arabic, Russian, Chinese, Portuguese).

---

## 🖼️ Slide 4: AI-Powered Learning Ecosystem
- **AI Placement Diagnostic (Hero Feature)**:
  - 5-step onboarding + adaptive evaluation generating a personalized 6-stage AI learning roadmap.
- **AI Learning Navigator**:
  - Generates customized **30-minute adaptive preparation plans** for real-world scenarios (*"Meeting an Indian diplomat tomorrow"*, *"Travelling to Delhi"*).
- **Hindi AI Voice Tutor**:
  - Real-time spoken dialogue with script view toggles (**Romanized Transliteration | Native Devanagari | Dual View**) and instant pronunciation feedback.

---

## 🖼️ Slide 5: LSRW Skill Tracking & CEFR Benchmark
- **4-Skill Precision Tracking**:
  - **L — Listening** (Audio comprehension & cadence recognition)
  - **S — Speaking** (Pronunciation, fluency, rhythm & pitch accuracy)
  - **R — Reading** (Devanagari script & vocabulary mastery)
  - **W — Writing** (Grammar, syntax & Subject-Object-Verb order)
- **CEFR Alignment**:
  - Accredited progress across A1, A2, B1, and B2 levels, with verified digital diploma generation and QR code verification.

---

## 🖼️ Slide 6: Indian Language Resource Hub
- **Centralized Aggregation**:
  - Single portal aggregating scattered learning materials from national institutions (*Central Institute of Indian Languages, Kendriya Hindi Sansthan, Sushma Swaraj Institute of Foreign Service, Delhi University*).
- **Multi-Faceted Search & Filters**:
  - Filter by Language, CEFR Level, LSRW Skill, Topic, Resource Type (Video, Audio, PDF), and Institution.
- **"Ask AI to Find Learning Resources"**:
  - Natural language query interface for instant resource discovery.

---

## 🖼️ Slide 7: Global Community & Language Propagation
- **Global Language Circles**:
  - Live virtual roundtables hosted by Indian diplomats and native linguists in local time zones (London, New York, Singapore, Tokyo, Dubai).
- **Global Creative Showcase**:
  - Learner poetry, essays, speech videos, and cultural stories celebrating language propagation worldwide.
- **Knowledge Return Network**:
  - Advanced international scholars contributing technical lectures and research summaries back to India in Indian languages.
